ABOUT THE PROJECT

Godrej Horizon, Dadar-Wadala

Rising proudly in the heart of Dadar-Wadala, Godrej Pinnacle offers a refined lifestyle where modern design, spacious living, and an exceptional location come together. Spread across a sprawling 5-acre development, the project is thoughtfully designed to balance open spaces, premium residences, and world-class amenities. With spacious homes featuring balconies and a magnificent 5-storey clubhouse, residents can enjoy a sophisticated urban lifestyle complemented by serene views of the Eastern Bay and Mumbai’s iconic skyline.
